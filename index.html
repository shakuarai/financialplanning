    What we help with

    Most people come to us with one question. We look at the whole picture, then start with whatever matters most to you right now.

    Ask about your situation

    • Clear debt and build a buffer

      A realistic plan for HECS-HELP, credit cards and car loans, plus an emergency fund so one bad month doesn't undo your progress.

    • Buy your first home

      Set a deposit target and timeline, find out which government schemes you qualify for, and work out what borrowing is comfortable, not just possible.

    • Make your super work harder

      Check your fund's fees and investment option, combine old accounts, and see whether extra contributions make sense on your income.

    • Start investing with a plan

      Choose a simple, low-cost mix of investments that suits your timeline, and set it up to run automatically each payday.

    • Get the insurance you need

      Income protection and life cover sized to your situation, starting with what your super fund already gives you.

    • Pay less tax, legally

      Salary sacrifice, deductions and investment structures that keep up with your income as it grows.

    How it works

    Four steps, and you only pay once you've seen the quote and said yes.

    1. A free 30-minute chat

      By video or at our Sydney office. Tell us where you are and where you want to be. You don't need to prepare anything.

    2. A fixed-fee quote

      If we can help, we tell you what your plan will cost before you commit. We don't take commissions or a cut of your savings.

    3. Your written plan

      Within two weeks you get a plan in plain English, starting with the first three things to do.

    4. A yearly check-in

      New job, new partner, new city: we review your plan every year, or sooner when something big changes.

    Questions people ask first

    Is the first chat really free?

    Yes. It takes 30 minutes, there's no obligation, and we won't ask for payment details.

    Do I need a lot of money to see a financial planner?

    No. Many clients come to us with a HECS debt and a few thousand dollars saved. Starting early does more than starting big.

    How much does a plan cost?

    It depends on how much the plan covers. After the first chat we give you a fixed quote in writing, and you decide whether to go ahead.

    Are you independent?

    We don't accept commissions from banks, super funds or insurers, so what we recommend isn't tied to what pays us.

    Can we meet online?

    Yes. Most first chats happen by video, and you can visit our office on Harbour Street whenever you prefer to meet in person.
